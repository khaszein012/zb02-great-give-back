# Lovable art prompt — Episode 3, all 41 images

One prompt, one paste. It generates every asset the game imports, at the exact
dimensions it displays them, named so the files drop straight into `src/assets/`.

**How to use:** paste sections 1–3 as a single message. Attach one existing image as a
style reference if you have one you like. Generate in the order given — the map and the
three characters must come first, because every later image references them.

---

## 1. What you are making

You are generating the complete illustration set for a children's storybook
English-learning game called **Detective Zabyr — Episode 3: The Great Give-Back**.

The story: a magpie in a tweed waistcoat has stolen every gadget in a small town called
Gadgeton. A boy detective and his floating camera friend collect them back one district
at a time.

I need **41 images** in a single consistent style. They will be displayed at the sizes
listed, so please honour the aspect ratios exactly. **Do not draw any text in any image**
— this is the single most important rule. Not on signs, not on books, not on screens, not
in the sky. Wherever writing would naturally go, paint abstract squiggles or leave it
blank.

**Output naming:** save each file with the exact filename given, e.g. `chapter-0.jpg`.

---

## 2. The style — applies to every image, without exception

> Children's storybook illustration in soft gouache and watercolour painted on textured
> paper. Warm palette: mustard yellow, terracotta, sage green, dusty blue, cream, with deep
> navy for shadows. Rounded friendly shapes, gentle soft outlines, no harsh black lines.
> Cosy, calm, welcoming mood — the feeling of a well-loved picture book. Even soft lighting,
> no dramatic or theatrical shadows. Flat-ish painted shading with visible paper grain and
> a hand-made feel. Slightly muted, never neon or glossy.
>
> **STRICT — no text anywhere.** Absolutely no lettering, words, captions, labels, numbers,
> signage, book titles, screen content, or speech bubbles. Any surface that would normally
> carry writing must show abstract squiggles or blank space instead.

Keep this wording identical in every generation. Paraphrasing between images is what makes
a set look inconsistent.

---

## 3. The characters — keep these descriptions identical every time

**ZABYR** — the hero
> A 10-year-old boy detective. Warm brown skin, short curly dark hair, a tan trench coat
> over a mustard-orange shirt, brown trousers, brown boots. A small brass magnifying glass
> in his coat pocket. Friendly, curious expression, slightly raised eyebrow. Calm and
> unbothered — he is never frightened.

**PIP** — the sidekick
> A small floating brass camera about the size of a sparrow. Two large round expressive
> eyes on the front, tiny thin arms, a soft warm glow around it. Adorable and friendly.
> Never mechanical, never scary, never a real camera with a lens barrel pointed at the
> viewer. Floats at shoulder height beside Zabyr.

**MISTER MAGPIE** — the villain
> A tall glossy black-and-white magpie standing upright like a person. Wearing a brown
> tweed waistcoat with small brass buttons. Often holding a paper bag of shiny objects or a
> small gadget. Smug, comical, pleased with himself. **Never menacing or frightening** —
> this is a game for young children, so he should read as a silly, greedy bird.

Keep Zabyr and Pip in the same pose vocabulary throughout: standing, looking, holding
something. Avoid running, jumping or action poses — his face drifts between images when he
is in motion.

## 9. Four Magic E pairs — 768×672 each

These are the most important technical constraint in the whole set.

Each pair shows the **same object in the same place from the same angle, with the same
lighting**, differing only in what the object is. The game fades from one to the other, so
a matched composition makes the change read as a little bit of magic. **Generate each pair
in a single prompt, side by side or in sequence, so the compositions match.**

| Pair | Short version | Long version |

| `magic-kit.jpg` → `magic-kite.jpg` | A small open toolbox with a few tools inside, sitting on grass. | Same grass, same light — a diamond-shaped kite on a string, floating at the same height where the box was. |
